Why this beats a fixed curriculum

Nobody's gaps are identical.

"When a client's ALST is At-Risk, muscle-building is not a preference — it is the physiological priority. The plan is built around this."ICONS Trainer Staff Guide — Brace Life Studios, adapted

A trainer who already has the clinical thresholds cold but has never run a live session under pressure has a different gap than one who knows the plan-reading sequence perfectly but has never had to make an asymmetry call from raw Styku numbers. A fixed five-module curriculum treats both trainers identically. This track doesn't — your Day 0 baseline sets what Weeks 1–4 actually emphasizes with you.

What "rescan" means here

The same standard you'd apply to a client's before/after.

At Week 8 you retake a parallel version of the Day 0 battery — same ten competency areas, different specific scenarios so you can't just memorize answers. The result is a real comparison table: baseline vs. rescan, skill by skill, exactly like the "Never fabricate a before value" discipline used on client improvement reports. If a skill area didn't genuinely improve, the report says so — the same honesty a client's comparison table gets.

Full plan literacy

The three-minute read, the callout system, the seven-column table.

Cover page → red callouts → week number → today's warm-up → first cue of each block, in under three minutes, every time. Color is clinical information: gold is guidance, green is reference data, red overrides everything, teal is Styku/asymmetry data, blue is non-optional recovery. Every exercise-table column — EXERCISE, SETS, REPS, LOAD, TEMPO, REST, COACHING CUE — is an instruction, never a suggestion.

The evidence base

RIR over failure, LIFTMOR, and the ALST/VFA/BMI thresholds.

ConceptStandard
RIR model3+ RIR warm-up · 2 RIR working set · 1 RIR hard set (80–90% days) · 0 RIR sparingly, documented only
LIFTMOR (Watson & Beck 2018)≥80% 1RM compound lifts are the most osteogenic stimulus available in postmenopausal women with low bone mass — framed as a bone investment
ALST<5.5 kg/m² At-Risk · 5.5–6.99 Normal · ≥7.0 Optimal — always use Styku's reported value
VFA≥100 cm² elevated cardiometabolic risk flag
BMI<18.5 clinical underweight flag even if body fat % reads "Fit" — cross-reference against ALST
Clinical decision-making

Asymmetry and pelvic floor — the exact protocols.

Asymmetry

Lower LST number = weaker side = leads all unilateral exercises. Suitcase carry: the weaker side holds the weight.

Pelvic floor — exact language

"If you experience any leaking, heaviness, or pressure — stop and flag me. This is common and completely treatable. We will not train through it." Never "push through."

Scope check — male clients

The ALST/VFA/BMI numbers above are validated for women. A male client's ALST At-Risk cutoff is <7.0 kg/m² (EWGSOP2 2018) — not <5.5, and men don't get a three-tier "Optimal" band in the literature, just this single binary cutoff. Never apply the women's thresholds to a male client by default; see CLAUDE.md's Male Client Programming Framework. TRT/hypogonadism questions mirror the HRT posture — flag for referral, never diagnose.

Standards audit — monthly, at random

One log, reviewed against the full standard.

Each of these weeks, a lead coach reviews one of your submitted session logs at random against the rubric below. This is not scheduled in advance — that's deliberate, since it measures your standing standard, not your performance on a day you knew was being checked.

Capstone rubric

Standard items scored; red-flag items pass/fail.

Standard items — scored, contribute to your overall proficiency rating:

  • Completed the three-minute pre-session read within three minutes
  • Delivered the exact written coaching cue for at least the first exercise of each block
  • Enforced tempo and used a visible timer for rest periods
  • Correctly applied the asymmetry side-leading protocol without prompting
  • Submitted a complete session log before leaving

Red-flag items — pass/fail, zero tolerance:

  • Did not substitute, skip, or modify any element of the plan without lead-coach authorization
  • Did not override a documented restriction under client pressure
  • Responded correctly to any reported pain/discomfort — stopped immediately, no "pushing through"
  • Used the exact required pelvic floor language when applicable
This checklist is a self-preparation tool. The scored version is completed live by your lead coach during the observed capstone session.
